AdventureWorks Comprehensive Sales Dashboard 📊

An end-to-end data analysis and visualization project built using Excel and Power Query, transforming raw operational data into actionable strategic insights through an advanced Galaxy Data Model and an interactive Dark Theme dashboard.

🚀 Project Overview

This project goes beyond visual aesthetics; it represents a complete End-to-End Data Analysis journey. Starting from raw datasets, the workflow covers data ingestion, cleaning, advanced modeling, DAX measure calculations, and executive-level visualization designed with a professional dark theme.

🛠️ Technical Architecture & Workflow

1. Data Cleaning & Transformation (Power Query)

Performed rigorous data cleaning to ensure data integrity and quality.

Handled missing values, standardized formats, and prepared tables for seamless integration and analysis.

2. Data Modeling (Galaxy Schema)

Designed an advanced Galaxy Model consisting of multiple Fact Tables and shared Dimension Tables.

Established robust relationships to handle complex business logic and flexible multi-dimensional analysis.

3. Resellers Analysis

Tracked and analyzed reseller performance across different business categories (e.g., Warehouse, Value Added Reseller, Specialty Bike Shop).

Evaluated purchasing behavior and revenue contribution per channel.

4. DAX & Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Developed powerful calculations and measures to track core metrics precisely:

Total Revenue

Net Profit

Average Order Value (AOV)

Number of Orders

📈 Key Insights

Revenue Growth: Demonstrates a strong upward trend in annual revenue from 2010 to 2013, culminating in a total sales volume of approximately $80.4 Million.

Geographical Distribution: The United States market dominates with over $53 Million in sales, followed by Canada at $14.3 Million, with promising expansion opportunities identified in European markets (France, Germany, UK).

Channel Performance: Warehouse and Value Added Resellers serve as the backbone of revenue generation compared to Specialty Bike Shops, reflecting a strong wholesale distribution network.

💡 Strategic Recommendations

European Market Expansion: Given the initial traction in Germany and France, targeted marketing campaigns should be deployed to capture a higher market share.

Wholesale Channel Incentives: Provide special offers and incentives to top-tier distribution partners (Warehouses and VARs) to maintain and maximize their high revenue return.

AOV Growth Strategies: Implement product bundling and cross-selling initiatives to increase the Average Order Value.
